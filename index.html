Japan has a very awesome scene for innovative and creative DTM. DTM stands for "desktop music" referring to the process of making music entirely on ones computerf. It's become short hand for a sort of independant music usually EDM and Techno adjacent music. The scene is most known for how expiramental it is with new genres and not sticking to generic motifs present in many electronic genres of music, as well as fusing lots of J-rock, J-pop and other more popular genre of music with niche forms of electronic music. Example of a song by a vocal and producer duo "endorfin" called Luminous Rage this a fusion song of J-rock and J-core (a broad genre term for the kinds of hardcore made in Japan often stylized by super saw leads). The duo in the song is vocals by 藍月なくる (Nakuru Aitsuki) and produced by sky_delta
